❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How frequently are campus circulars updated?

Official circulars and examination notifications are published daily as soon as they are approved by the relevant school dean or registrar.

What do the deadline countdown colors indicate?

Green indicates over 7 days remaining, orange indicates 3 to 7 days, and red indicates 2 days or fewer before the deadline expires.

How can faculty publish a circular?

Faculty members logged in with the Teacher role can click "+ Post Notice" in the notices toolbar to publish official notices with attachments.

Can I download circular attachments offline?

Yes. Click on any notice to view details, then click "Download" on the attachment row to save the document to your device.

Does this portal work in offline / private mode?

Yes. The entire system is engineered with client-side storage fallbacks so it functions smoothly even in private browsing or offline modes.
